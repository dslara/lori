# No-malus progression regime

Lori's design docs originally punished failure with streak resets, review penalties, and weakness debuffs. Dani vetoed punishment: studying must stay calm, so nothing ever subtracts. Streak freezes and resumes, late reviews pile visibly without touching SM-2, ignored weaknesses only invite drills, combos grant bonus or nothing. Retention is carried by narrative curiosity instead, with chapter cliffhangers as the primary driver.

## Considered Options

Punitive stakes with decay multipliers, rejected for clashing with calm study and risking abandonment.

## Consequences

Economy is bonus-only, so all progression math assumes multipliers from 1 upward. SRS urgency must come from visibility, never from ease-factor cuts. Horror and other dark skins stay gloomy about the world, never shaming about the paused player.
